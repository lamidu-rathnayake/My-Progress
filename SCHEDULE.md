# Schedule

All times are local to Sri Lanka (Asia/Colombo).

## Current phase — before the semester starts

The semester has not started. The lecture and practical hours below are expected future commitments, not current scheduled classes. For now, try two 25–40 minute algebra sessions on rested evenings and a 10-minute weekly review around work. Any additional weekend study is optional; do not fill all time that will later be needed for university. Reassess when the start date and timetable are confirmed.

## Provisional rhythm once the semester starts

Do not assign fixed weekday names until the timetable is available. Protect sleep, meals, and recovery before allocating optional study.

- Lecture evenings: no additional study requirement.
- Up to two non-lecture evenings: one 25–40 minute block each, only if energy allows. Give the earliest deadline priority; otherwise practise algebra.
- Weekend: at most one optional 30–45 minute catch-up block after recovery, if class demands permit.
- Weekly review: 10 minutes to record evidence, check deadlines, and choose the next small outcome.

This is approximately 1–2.25 hours of provisional independent work, including academic preparation and mathematics, not an extra project quota. Actual assessments may require more time; revise optional commitments when deadlines are known. Do not assume this budget is sufficient for every academic week.

## Workload rules

1. Urgent assessments and exam preparation take available study capacity first.
2. Keep mathematics consistent through short sessions where feasible.
3. FieldSync and specialization share any remaining capacity; choose one small task rather than scheduling both by default.
4. During heavy weeks, pause personal project work. During exhausted weeks, rest and revise the plan without creating catch-up debt.
5. Do not rely on commute study or late-night sessions. Reassess after two weeks of actual energy and timetable data.

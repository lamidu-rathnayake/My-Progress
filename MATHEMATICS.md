## 2. Algebra foundations

### 2.1 Expressions and notation

- [ ] Variables, constants, coefficients, terms, and substitution.

### 2.2 Linear equations and rearranging formulas

- [ ] One-step and multi-step equations, including brackets and fractions.

- [ ] Solve linear inequalities and represent solutions on a number line.

- [ ] Read interval notation and simple absolute-value inequalities.

### 2.4 Polynomials and factorization

- [ ] Simplify algebraic fractions while retaining excluded denominator values.

- [ ] Solve by factorization, completing the square, and the quadratic formula.

### 2.6 Simultaneous equations

- [ ] Solve two linear equations by substitution and elimination.

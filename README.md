# Engineering Journey

A lightweight learning journal for a Software Engineering undergraduate at SLTC Research University, entering Year 3 in 2026 and working as an Application Support Engineer in banking software.

## Direction and priorities

Primary specialization: **Intelligent Systems & Applied AI/ML Engineering**, with interests in systems programming, intelligent devices, signal processing, and audio technology.

Core technologies: Python, C++, Linux, NumPy, SciPy, scikit-learn, PyTorch, DSP, real-time processing, ONNX Runtime, and Edge AI. React, TypeScript, SQL, and C#/.NET remain supporting engineering skills. These are a direction, not simultaneous study commitments.

University assessments and mathematics come first. Learn prerequisites before advanced topics, prefer free resources, and use practical exercises with measurable outcomes. Adapt project work to academic commitments and protect sleep and recovery.
